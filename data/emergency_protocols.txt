MedNova Hospital Chennai — Emergency Department Protocols

Emergency Department Overview
The MedNova Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week,
with a dedicated team of emergency physicians, nurses, and support staff.
The department has 20 acute care bays, 4 resuscitation bays, and a 6-bed
short-stay observation unit. The ED is supported by an in-house trauma team,
on-call surgical and medical specialists, and round-the-clock radiology and
laboratory services.

Triage Protocol
All patients arriving at the Emergency Department are triaged within 5
minutes of arrival using a 5-level acuity scale.
Level 1 — Resuscitation, immediate care, life-threatening conditions.
Level 2 — Emergent, care within 10 minutes, high-risk conditions.
Level 3 — Urgent, care within 30 minutes, stable but concerning conditions.
Level 4 — Less urgent, care within 60 minutes.
Level 5 — Non-urgent, care within 120 minutes.

Trauma Activation Criteria
A full trauma team activation is called for patients with systolic blood
pressure below 90 mmHg, GCS less than 9, penetrating injury to head/neck/
torso/proximal extremities, gunshot wounds, fall from height greater than
6 metres, or paralysis. The trauma team responds within 5 minutes and
includes a trauma surgeon, ED physician, anaesthetist, and trauma nurses.

Stroke Protocol
Step 1 — Suspected stroke patient is triaged as Level 1.
Step 2 — Stroke team is paged immediately on arrival.
Step 3 — Non-contrast CT brain is performed within 25 minutes of arrival.
Step 4 — Neurologist reviews imaging and patient within 45 minutes.
Step 5 — IV thrombolysis is administered within 60 minutes of arrival if
indicated and within the 4.5-hour window.
Step 6 — Mechanical thrombectomy is considered for large vessel occlusion
within 6 hours of symptom onset.

Sepsis Protocol
Patients meeting SIRS criteria with suspected infection are screened for
sepsis. The sepsis bundle is initiated within 1 hour: blood cultures before
antibiotics, broad-spectrum IV antibiotics, IV crystalloid 30 ml/kg for
hypotension or lactate above 4 mmol/L, serum lactate measurement, and
vasopressors for persistent hypotension.

Cardiac Arrest Protocol
Code Blue is announced overhead and the resuscitation team responds within
2 minutes. CPR follows the current AHA Advanced Cardiac Life Support
guidelines. The crash cart is brought to the bedside, and a recorder
documents all interventions, medications, and rhythm changes. Post-arrest
care includes targeted temperature management for unconscious survivors.

Disaster and Mass Casualty Plan
The MedNova ED activates the Hospital Incident Command System for mass
casualty events. Triage shifts to the START protocol (Simple Triage and
Rapid Treatment). Additional staff are recalled, elective surgeries are
postponed, and inpatient beds are made available through expedited
discharges.
