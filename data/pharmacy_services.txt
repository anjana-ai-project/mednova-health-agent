MedNova Hospital Chennai — Pharmacy Services

Pharmacy Operating Hours
The main pharmacy at MedNova Hospital is open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
The outpatient pharmacy counter is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
Emergency medicines are dispensed round the clock from the inpatient pharmacy.

Prescription Policy
All medicines are dispensed strictly against valid prescriptions from
MedNova registered doctors. Prescriptions are valid for 30 days from
the date of issue. Controlled substances require special prescription
forms and are dispensed only to the patient or immediate family member
with valid ID proof.

Medicine Availability
MedNova pharmacy stocks over 2000 medicines covering all specialisations.
Generic medicines are available as cost-effective alternatives to branded drugs.
Patients are informed when a medicine is out of stock and alternatives are suggested.
Critical medicines for ICU and emergency are maintained with buffer stock of 30 days.

Discharge Medicines
Discharge medicines are prepared by the pharmacy within 1 hour of receiving
the discharge prescription. Patients are counselled on dosage, timing, and
side effects before collecting medicines. Home delivery of medicines is
available within 10 km of the hospital for a nominal fee.

Drug Interactions and Safety
MedNova pharmacy uses a computerised drug interaction checking system.
All high-alert medicines are double-checked before dispensing.
Chemotherapy drugs are prepared in a dedicated sterile unit.
Cold chain medicines are stored and transported under controlled conditions.

Medicines are covered under cashless insurance for inpatients.
OPD medicine bills can be submitted to insurance for reimbursement.
Pharmacy bills are itemised and provided on request.
