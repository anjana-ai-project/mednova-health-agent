MedNova Hospital Chennai — Cardiology Protocols

Cardiology Department Overview
The Cardiology department at MedNova Hospital Chennai provides comprehensive
cardiac care including diagnostic, interventional, and rehabilitative services.
The department operates a 24-hour Chest Pain Unit and a dedicated Cardiac ICU
with 10 beds.

Heart Attack Protocol
Step 1 — Patient presenting with chest pain is triaged immediately.
Step 2 — 12-lead ECG is performed within 10 minutes of arrival.
Step 3 — Cardiac biomarkers including Troponin I and CK-MB are drawn.
Step 4 — Aspirin 325mg is administered if not contraindicated.
Step 5 — Cardiologist is notified within 15 minutes of ECG confirmation.
Step 6 — Primary PCI is performed within 90 minutes of first medical contact.

Cardiac ICU Admission Criteria
Patients admitted to the Cardiac ICU include those with acute myocardial
infarction, unstable angina with haemodynamic compromise, life-threatening
arrhythmias, acute heart failure with pulmonary oedema, post cardiac arrest,
and post cardiac surgery requiring monitoring.

Echocardiography Services
Transthoracic echocardiography is available Monday to Saturday 8AM to 6PM.
Emergency echocardiography is available 24 hours via on-call technician.
Transesophageal echocardiography requires prior appointment and anaesthesia
clearance. Results are reported by a cardiologist within 2 hours for emergency
studies and 24 hours for routine studies.

Cardiac Rehabilitation
MedNova offers a structured 12-week cardiac rehabilitation programme for
patients following heart attack, bypass surgery, or heart failure.
Sessions are conducted three times per week in the physiotherapy department.
Each session includes supervised exercise, dietary counselling, and
psychological support.

Common Cardiology Medications at MedNova
Aspirin — antiplatelet therapy for all CAD patients.
Clopidogrel — dual antiplatelet therapy post PCI.
Atorvastatin — lipid lowering for all cardiac patients.
Metoprolol — beta blocker for heart failure and post MI.
Ramipril — ACE inhibitor for heart failure and hypertension.
Furosemide — diuretic for fluid overload in heart failure.
