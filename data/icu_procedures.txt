MedNova Hospital Chennai — ICU Procedures and Protocols

ICU Admission Criteria
Patients requiring ICU admission include those with acute respiratory failure,
haemodynamic instability, post-operative monitoring after major surgery,
severe sepsis or septic shock, acute neurological emergencies, and multi-organ
dysfunction syndrome.

ICU Discharge Criteria
A patient is considered ready for ICU discharge when haemodynamic stability
is achieved without vasopressor support, oxygen saturation is maintained above
95% on low-flow oxygen, the patient is alert and oriented, and urine output
exceeds 0.5ml/kg/hour for 6 consecutive hours.

ICU Discharge Procedure
Step 1 — Attending physician issues discharge order after ward round assessment.
Step 2 — ICU nurse completes transfer summary including vitals, medications,
and nursing notes.
Step 3 — Receiving ward is notified at least 2 hours before transfer.
Step 4 — Patient is transferred with continuous monitoring until handover.
Step 5 — Receiving ward nurse signs handover checklist.
Step 6 — ICU bed is sanitised and prepared for next admission within 1 hour.

Visiting Hours — ICU
Visiting hours in the ICU are restricted to 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM and
5:00 PM to 6:00 PM daily. Maximum 2 visitors per patient. Children below
12 years are not permitted in the ICU. Visitors must wear PPE provided
at the ICU entrance.

ICU Infection Control
All staff must perform hand hygiene before and after patient contact.
Ventilator bundles are implemented for all intubated patients.
Central line bundles are followed for all central venous catheters.
Daily sedation vacations and spontaneous breathing trials are conducted
for ventilated patients.
