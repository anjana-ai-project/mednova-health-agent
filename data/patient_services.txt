MedNova Hospital Chennai — Patient Services Guide

Outpatient Department (OPD) Services
OPD services are available Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Sunday OPD is available for emergency consultations only from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
Patients must register at the front desk before consulting a doctor.
Online appointments can be booked via the MedNova patient portal or by calling
the helpline at 044-2200-1000.

Inpatient Admission Process
Step 1 — Doctor issues admission advice after consultation.
Step 2 — Patient proceeds to admission desk with doctor's advice and ID proof.
Step 3 — Insurance pre-authorisation is obtained if applicable.
Step 4 — Patient is allotted a bed based on ward availability and medical condition.
Step 5 — Nursing staff conducts initial assessment within 30 minutes of admission.
Step 6 — Attending doctor visits within 2 hours of admission.

General Ward Visiting Hours
General ward visiting hours are 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily.
Maximum 2 visitors per patient at any time.
Visitors are requested to maintain silence and switch mobile phones to silent mode.

Discharge Process
Step 1 — Attending doctor issues discharge summary and prescription.
Step 2 — Nursing staff prepares discharge checklist.
Step 3 — Patient proceeds to billing counter for final settlement.
Step 4 — Pharmacy dispenses discharge medicines.
Step 5 — Patient exits after collecting discharge summary and medicines.

Billing and Insurance
MedNova Hospital accepts cashless treatment under all major insurance providers.
TPA desk is available 24 hours for insurance queries.
Detailed billing statements are provided on request.
EMI options are available for bills above Rs. 50,000.

Cafeteria and Facilities
Patient cafeteria is open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
Diet meals as per doctor's advice are provided to inpatients.
Wheelchair and stretcher services are available at all entry points.
Free Wi-Fi is available in all patient rooms and waiting areas.
